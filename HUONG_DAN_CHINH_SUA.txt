HUONG DAN CHINH SUA WEBSITE DU LICH - DI DAU DO

1. Mo website:
   - Giai nen file ZIP.
   - Mo thu muc di_dau_do_travel.
   - Nhan dup index.html de xem website.

2. Doi ten website:
   - Mo cac file HTML bang Notepad.
   - Tim cum tu "DI DAU DO" va thay bang ten moi.
   - Luu bang Ctrl + S.

3. Doi mau sac:
   - Mo css/style.css bang Notepad.
   - Tim phan :root o gan dau file.
   - Sua cac ma mau HEX, vi du #315c4d.
   - Luu bang Ctrl + S va tai lai trang web.

4. Sua noi dung tung trang:
   - index.html: Trang chu.
   - destinations.html: Diem den.
   - itinerary.html: Lich trinh.
   - experiences.html: Trai nghiem.
   - about.html: Gioi thieu.

5. Sua hieu ung va chuc nang:
   - js/main.js

Luu y:
   - Khong xoa dau ngoac < > trong HTML.
   - Khong xoa dau { } va dau ; trong CSS/JavaScript.
   - Nen giu nguyen ten va vi tri cac thu muc css, js.
   - Nen tao mot ban sao truoc khi sua.
